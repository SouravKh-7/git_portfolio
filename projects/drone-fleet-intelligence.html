Environmental & Health-Aware Drone Fleet Intelligence

A data-engineering, optimization, digital-twin, and bounded-Agentic-AI project for assigning and routing inspection UAVs under changing environmental, mission, communication, battery, and component-health conditions.

Evidence boundary: this page describes a staged research and implementation plan. It does not claim a deployed drone system, field result, hardware-in-the-loop test, or autonomous flight controller.

01 · Core question

When does the shortest route become the wrong route?

The geographically shortest mission may stop being the safest, most energy-efficient, most reliable, or most productive once weather, battery reserve, motor or bearing health, communication, fleet workload, inspection priority, and return-to-base risk are considered.

The portfolio contribution is not a claim to invent these research fields. It is a data-engineering and enterprise-systems synthesis that makes interfaces, evidence, assumptions, trade-offs, failure modes, and measurable outcomes inspectable.

03 · Trusted data plane

The drone is a physical endpoint. The enterprise still needs trustworthy state.

Mission / business

Intent and priority

Mission ID, inspection target, due time, customer or asset impact, payload need.

Vehicle state

Feasible availability

Position, velocity, payload, availability, battery SoC / SoH, reserve threshold.

Component health

Condition and stress

Motor current, vibration, temperature, bearing-health score, maintenance state.

Environment

Weather exposure

Wind vector, gust, temperature, precipitation, visibility, validity window.

Airspace / geography

Route feasibility

Targets, terrain, obstacles, no-fly cells, geofences, charging locations.

Communication

Connectivity risk

Link quality, coverage estimate, packet loss, communication requirement.

Fleet operations

System-level constraints

Task queue, charging slots, maintenance holds, conflicts, separation, workload.

Inspection evidence

Closed-loop outcome

Captures, detections, defect candidates, confidence, and evidence quality.

04 · Deterministic baseline

Give AI something transparent to beat, explain, or coordinate.

route_score = wD·distance + wT·time + wE·energy + wW·weather + wH·health + wC·communication + wR·mission risk + hard-constraint penalties
Planner comparisons
PlannerUsesPurpose
Shortest path / nearest availableDistance onlySimple reference baseline
Energy-awareDistance + vehicle energy modelShows why shortest does not always mean least energy
Weather-awareEnergy + wind / gust / temperatureShows environmental adaptation
Health-awareBattery SoH + component stress + reserveShows asset-condition-aware operation
Fleet-awareAllocation + charging + workload + route conflictsMeasures system productivity, not one vehicle
Agent-assistedThe same deterministic planners and toolsTests operator interaction and decision speed without replacing feasibility checks

Hard constraints remain outside the LLM

Geofences and no-fly areas, separation, battery return reserve, vehicle availability, required payload, maintenance lockout, maximum weather thresholds, mandatory deadlines, and controller or actuator limits.

05 · Where AI belongs

AI produces evidence, candidates, and explanations—not permission to fly.

  1. 01Parse operator intent
  2. 02Fetch trusted state
  3. 03Call allocator & planner
  4. 04Call simulator
  5. 05Compare candidates
  6. 06Policy check
  7. 07Human review
  8. 08Publish structured plan
Anomaly / health ML

Detect condition risk

Estimate degradation and derive risk features. The output is evidence—not permission to fly.

Energy / route ML

Predict candidate cost

Benchmark against transparent physical or heuristic baselines and keep uncertainty visible.

Computer vision

Attach inspection evidence

A defect candidate remains a candidate, not a final engineering diagnosis.

Reinforcement learning

Later simulator research

Training and evaluation stay inside simulation; safety constraints remain external.

LLM / Agentic AI

Interpret and coordinate

Translate intent, retrieve evidence, call approved tools, and explain trade-offs. Never act as autopilot, trajectory stabilizer, or motor controller.

07 · Practical implementation path

Data engineering and deterministic simulation first.

Phase 1

Deterministic laptop simulation

Synthetic typed mission and fleet data, DuckDB + Parquet, a graph model, explicit heuristic or OR-Tools allocation, replayable simulation, FastAPI, and a static evidence view.

Next
Phase 2

Software-in-the-loop boundary

Connect the structured mission plan to PX4 or ArduPilot SITL through a MAVLink adapter, then return simulator telemetry into the trusted event pipeline.

Planned
Phase 3

Research AI

Compare health and weather prediction, a high-level tool-using agent, and later learning-based replanning against the deterministic baseline inside simulation.
