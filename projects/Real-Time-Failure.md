# Health-Aware Autonomous Drone System

## Overview

This project develops a practical health-aware path-planning system for autonomous drones. It uses real-time component condition (motor and bearing health), navigation, and sensor data to choose routes that lower mechanical stress and reduce failure risk. The goal is simpler: make drone missions safer and more reliable while surfacing clear maintenance actions for operators.

---

## Motivation

Drones operating in inspection, surveillance, agriculture, logistics, and industrial environments are often exposed to varying loads and environmental conditions. Over time, critical components may experience degradation that can affect system performance and reliability.

This project is motivated by the idea that autonomous systems should not only understand their external environment but also be aware of their internal operating condition. By incorporating health-related information into mission planning and operational decisions, it may be possible to improve safety, reliability, and overall mission success.

---

## Project Focus

The project investigates the integration of:

* Real-time system health monitoring
* Mission risk assessment
* Adaptive operational strategies
* Reliability-oriented decision support

Rather than focusing solely on fault detection, the objective is to explore how health information can be used to support safer and more informed autonomous behavior.
