Manufacturing & Retail Supply Chain Lakehouse

A planned Databricks project for joining manufacturing, supplier, warehouse, inventory, sales and return data.

Repository ↗Current evidencePortfolio home
01 · Problem

What problem am I solving?

Manufacturing, warehouse and retail systems use different keys and grains. Their data also arrives and changes at different times. The planned pipeline will keep history, separate bad rows and check quantities between stages.

02 · Current state

What exists now?

A Python source-contract type, one contract test, CI setup, project folders and design notes.

Not built yet: Bronze, Silver and Gold pipelines, Delta tables, Databricks jobs, data generators, benchmarks, analytics results and deployment.

03 · Architecture

Target Medallion flow

  1. Synthetic sources
  2. Bronze contracts
  3. Silver conformance
  4. Quality & reconciliation
  5. Gold products
04 · Data engineering design

Incremental and replayable by contract

The roadmap separates event time from arrival time and covers source watermarks, hashes, idempotent Delta merges, late-data windows, bounded backfills, quarantine, freshness and publication gates.

05 · Data model

Conformed manufacturing and retail entities

Proposed entities include suppliers, products, plants, warehouses, stores, customers, purchase orders and receipts, manufacturing orders and output, inventory movements and snapshots, retail orders, sales and returns. Grain and history rules must be finalized before transforms.

06 · Reliability and quality controls

Quality, quarantine and reconciliation

  • Schema, null, uniqueness, reference, domain, chronology, quantity, amount and freshness rules.
  • Rejected payloads retain rule, run and reprocessing metadata.
  • Layer counts and business quantities reconcile before Gold publication.
  • Repeat-run and backfill invariants require explicit tests.

08 · Failure scenarios

Planned failure lab

Missing batches, duplicates, schema drift, unknown references, partial writes, late and out-of-order changes, deletions, checkpoint loss, skew and failed reconciliation. No recovery result is claimed.

09 · Business relevance

One traceable data foundation across operations

The intended Gold layer will support supplier delivery, production, inventory, fulfillment, sales and return analysis. No dashboard, KPI result, cost saving or deployed business outcome is claimed.
